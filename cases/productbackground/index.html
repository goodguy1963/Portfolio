Product­Background

AI scenes & product placement

Build the environment first. Then place the product. ProductBackground combines controlled lifestyle backplates with Product Studio, where Nano Banana Pro places a referenced product into the selected scene. Review the composition, extend it horizontally or vertically, upscale, and export a PNG for final finishing.

01 Prework

Public model APIs came before the product.

Before building ProductBackground, I published and iterated on image-generation, image-to-image, enhancement, and upscaling models through Replicate. That work made model packaging, input and output contracts, runtime constraints, and remote inference tangible—and became technical prework for the more controlled end-to-end product.

02 Problem

Approve the world before placing the product.

Scene direction and product placement need separate decisions. ProductBackground first generates the atmosphere, architecture, materials, lighting, people, wildlife, food, and props around an empty foreground. The user can approve that environment before introducing a product reference in Product Studio.

The clear surface and frontal perspective create a placement area for products roughly 10–35 cm tall. Product Studio adds AI-assisted composition inside the app, while PNG export keeps manual placement and detailed retouching available in Photoshop or another editor.

03 System

From scene direction to a reviewed product image.

Guided generation and free scene prompts cover seven building blocks: character, animals, food, objects, surface, lighting, and location. Users generate a backplate, place a product reference in Product Studio, review the result, and apply outpainting or upscaling when the delivery format needs it. PNG export carries the selected image into final retouching.

04 Product Studio

The product now enters the workflow inside the app.

Product Studio accepts one required product reference and an optional second view of the same product. Real-world dimensions and placement notes guide Nano Banana Pro as it composes the product into the chosen backplate, including scale, perspective, lighting, contact shadows, and reflections.

The original scene and the placed result remain distinct review steps. The workflow aims to preserve product identity, but users still need to inspect labels, logos, geometry, colours, materials, and invented details before publication.
